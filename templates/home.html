About SNS College

SNS College stands as a beacon of academic excellence in Nepal. Under the leadership of Campus Admin Er. Sushmita Marasini and our dedicated Department Deans, the college provides cutting-edge laboratory infrastructure, comprehensive curriculum frameworks, and transparent grading standards.

Our faculties ensure that every student receives individualized academic mentorship, research opportunities, and practical training across Kathmandu, Pokhara, and Lalitpur campuses.
